EnvelopeCoder

Addressed envelopes with correct USPS barcodes, printed from one window. Nine envelope sizes, Intelligent Mail and POSTNET barcodes, Contacts integration, and optional address normalization.

Free download. Requires macOS 14.0 or later. Every feature is free for 21 days; envelope printing and address normalization stay free after that.
